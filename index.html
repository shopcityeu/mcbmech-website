How booking works

Four simple steps.

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Call, WhatsApp or email us with your registration and the work you need.

  2. 02

    We confirm time and price

    We agree a time that suits you and confirm the price for your vehicle before we start.

  3. 03

    Approve before we start

    If we find more work, you get a clear quotation first. Nothing extra is charged without your approval.

  4. 04

    Collect

    We let you know when it is ready and explain what was done, with a clear invoice.
