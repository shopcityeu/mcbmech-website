Privacy policy

Last updated: 06/11/2026

This policy explains how MCB ("MCB", "we") collects and uses personal data when you use our website and services. We are the controller of your personal data under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, telephone number, address and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Vehicle details: registration, make, model, year, VIN, mileage, MOT and other dates you choose to enter, photographs and documents.
  • Booking, quotation, repair, invoice and payment records. Card details are handled by our payment provider and never stored by us.
  • Messages you send us and records of our communication with you.
  • Technical data needed to keep the service secure: sign-in sessions, IP address and browser type.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

  • To provide the services you book, including reminders about your own vehicle (performance of a contract).
  • To keep invoices and financial records as required by law (legal obligation).
  • To keep the platform secure and prevent fraud, and to improve our service (legitimate interests).
  • To send marketing, only if you opt in. You can withdraw consent at any time in your account or through the unsubscribe link (consent).

Who we share it with

We share data only with providers who help us run the service, such as hosting, email, SMS and payment providers, under contracts that protect your data. We do not sell your data. If any provider processes data outside the UK, we use approved safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while it is active. Invoices and financial records are kept for at least six years to meet HMRC requirements. When you delete your account, other data is deleted or anonymised, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, object to processing, and receive your data in a portable format. You can download your data and request deletion from the Privacy section of your account, or contact us at the email address on our contact page. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Photographs and uploads

Location data (EXIF/GPS) is removed from photographs when they are uploaded. Uploads are stored privately and are only visible to you and authorised staff.

Changes

We will update this policy when our practices change and show the date of the latest version above.
